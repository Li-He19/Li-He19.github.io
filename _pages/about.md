I am a Master of Science student in **Computer Science** at the **University of Victoria**, supervised by [Prof. Jianping Pan](https://webhome.cs.uvic.ca/~pan/). My work spans **applied AI**, **distributed machine learning**, and **full-stack software development**.

Currently, I am a **Full-Stack Developer Co-op** with the **Ministry of Housing and Municipal Affairs**, where I develop LLM-powered capabilities for a production grant management platform. My work includes natural-language-to-SQL reporting, document summarization and analysis, and automated application scoring. I am especially interested in building reliable AI tools that make complex data and workflows accessible to non-technical users.

My research focuses on the performance of large-scale distributed training systems, particularly communication bottlenecks in mixture-of-experts models. Across both research and engineering, I enjoy turning technical ideas into practical, well-integrated systems.

Selected Projects
======

1. **AI Features for a Grant Management System (Unity)**
   *Technologies: Python, Flask, Angular, PostgreSQL, LangChain, Azure OpenAI, Vector Database, Metabase, Azure DevOps, .NET*
   - Built and deployed an LLM-powered system that translates natural-language questions into SQL, enabling non-technical users to query PostgreSQL data.
   - Developed pipelines for document summarization, application analysis, and automated scoring to support grant evaluation.
   - Integrated a Flask and Angular reporting module into an existing .NET platform, with interactive results visualized in Metabase.

2. **Traffic Pattern Analysis and Comparison of Distributed Deep Learning Models**
   *Technologies: PyTorch (DistributedDataParallel), ASTRA-sim, Slurm, NVIDIA DGX-2, Google TPU*
   - Designed and ran distributed training experiments for models including VGG16 and GPT-3 on Digital Research Alliance of Canada HPC clusters.
   - Used ASTRA-sim to evaluate training performance across network topologies and identify communication bottlenecks in fully connected layers.

3. **Large-Scale Text Embedding and Clustering**
   *Technologies: Python, Pinecone, Sentence Transformers, Scikit-learn (PCA, K-Means), KeyBERT*
   - Built a semantic search pipeline using Sentence Transformers and the Pinecone vector database.
   - Applied PCA and K-Means to organize related documents and used KeyBERT to enrich metadata with automatically extracted keywords.

4. **Markov-Chain Analysis of the RED Algorithm**
   *Technologies: Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, Computer Networking, Performance Modeling, Markov Chains*
   - Developed a Markov-chain model to simulate the queueing behavior of the Random Early Detection (RED) congestion-control algorithm.
   - Compared linear and exponential RED and visualized their throughput and latency under simulated network congestion.

5. **Maternal Health Risk Prediction**
   *Technologies: Python, Scikit-learn (SGD, Random Forest), TensorFlow, Pandas*
   - Built and compared SGD, random forest, and neural-network classifiers for predicting maternal health risk levels from physiological data.
   - Evaluated model performance to identify the most effective approach for early risk detection.
